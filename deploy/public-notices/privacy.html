WACRM privacy notice

Sqkii Pte. Ltd. operates WACRM, a workspace CRM. This notice covers people who sign up for or use WACRM and people who communicate with a workspace through a connected channel. You do not need a WACRM account to contact us about your information. This notice is not for the private KC Lead Tracker.

What we use and why

  • Accounts and security. We use your name, email address, workspace membership and role, and sign-in and security records to create accounts, verify email, manage access, and protect the service.
  • Connected WhatsApp accounts. When connection is available and an authorized member chooses to connect, we use business account and phone identifiers, permissions, and connection credentials to verify and operate that connection. We record connection changes, including disconnections.
  • Conversations. An enabled connection can deliver messages, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, delivery or read status, and attachment information to WACRM. We use this information to show conversations to authorized members of the relevant workspace. A workspace can also add information to manage its follow-up. Connecting a number does not authorize an address-book import or a request for past chats. Those features are not part of the initial rollout.
  • Temporary delivery records. We may briefly retain supported, authenticated WhatsApp notifications that are not yet associated with a workspace to handle delivery and protect connections. These records are not available in a workspace inbox. We keep separate records to recognize repeat deliveries and enforce disconnections.

Who handles it

The organization you message decides why it communicates with you and how it uses its workspace records. Sqkii operates the service and manages access and security. Workspace records are available to people authorized for that workspace. Sqkii runs WACRM on a self-hosted server. Google Workspace sends account verification emails, and Cloudflare helps deliver and protect the website. Meta handles WhatsApp under its own notices; authorized WhatsApp connections exchange information with Meta. Suppliers receive the information needed for the service they provide.

Retention and requests

Temporary delivery payloads are subject to scheduled cleanup. Some records, such as disconnection evidence, need to be kept separately to prevent a stopped connection from resuming. Workspace data, account records and backups are separate from the temporary delivery store. How long we keep information depends on its purpose, applicable requirements, and any relevant agreement. Removing a live record does not necessarily remove backup copies at the same time. Disconnecting WhatsApp is not a request to delete information already held.

Email team@sqkii.com with a privacy question or request. See the WACRM data deletion instructions for what to include.
