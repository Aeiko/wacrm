WACRM data deletion instructions

Email team@sqkii.com with the subject WACRM data deletion request. Sqkii Pte. Ltd. handles these requests. You do not need a WACRM account.

  • If you use WACRM: Give the email address you use to sign in, the workspace name if known, and what you want removed. Closing a login, deleting workspace information, and removing data from a WhatsApp connection are different requests.
  • If you messaged a business using WACRM: Name the business if you know it. Give an approximate date range and enough information to find the conversation, such as the WhatsApp number you used. If you do not know the workspace name, you can still write to us.

Do not send passwords, sign-in links, WhatsApp access tokens, or someone else’s information. We may ask for limited information to verify that you are entitled to make the request. If the request concerns a workspace’s records, we may need to coordinate with that organization. We will explain what we can remove and whether anything needs to be kept.

Signing out or closing your login does not, by itself, delete messages or attachments from a workspace. Disconnecting WhatsApp does not delete them either. Removing a live record may not remove backup copies at the same time. Deleting information from WACRM does not remove copies held independently by Meta, a message recipient, or the workspace outside WACRM. See the WACRM privacy notice for how the service handles information.
